## Technical strengths worth interviewing for

**Coding-agent evaluation:** Inspect model-generated changes for objective alignment, correctness, interface contracts, schema fidelity, testability, and unexpected side effects.

**Model evaluation design:** Hold the task constant across independently served model configurations; distinguish results caused by model reasoning from tool problems, retrieval quality, rate limits, or failed artifact persistence.

**Product engineering:** Build developer and operator interfaces, backend APIs, data pipelines, and model orchestration components.

**Enterprise domain depth:** Ground engineering choices in purchase orders, inventory, financial records, ERP workflows, approval controls, data integration, and traceable business behavior.
